# Pricing and Availability  (App Store → Monetization)

Mandatory. Cannot submit without a price and at least one territory.

---

## Price

    Free  (Price Tier 0)

Assumed rather than asked: TimeBox is MIT licensed, the source is public, and
the notarized DMG is already a free download. Charging on the App Store while
giving the same binary away on GitHub would be a strange proposition.

To change it later: Pricing and Availability → Price Schedule. Going from free
to paid is allowed; going from paid to free is too, but you cannot refund past
purchasers.

---

## Availability

    All countries and regions

No reason to restrict. The app has no region-specific content, no legal
exposure, and is English-only — which limits discovery, not eligibility.

---

## Distribution

- **Pre-Orders** — off.
- **Distribution method** — public on the Mac App Store.
- **Volume Purchase Program (Apple Business Manager)** — leave at the default.
  Free apps are eligible; it costs nothing and needs no configuration.

---

## In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Nothing to configure. TimeBox has neither, and the sections can be left empty.

---

## Before this section will save

The **Free Apps agreement** must be active under Business → Agreements, Tax, and
Banking. If Pricing refuses to save, that is almost always why. A free app needs
no tax or banking details, but the agreement itself must be accepted.
