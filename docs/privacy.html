Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

TimeBox collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. Nothing you type into it leaves your Mac.

This is not a promise about intent — it is a property of the program. TimeBox contains no networking code at all: no HTTP client, no telemetry library, no auto-updater, and no third-party SDK. The source is public and can be read in full at github.com/gigiheristiawan/timebox.

What TimeBox stores, and where

Your tasks, time blocks, and settings are written to a single SQLite database on your own Mac. Nothing else is stored anywhere.

Mac App Store version
~/Library/Containers/xyz.gigiheristiawan.timebox/Data/Library/Application Support/
Direct download version
~/Library/Application Support/xyz.gigiheristiawan.timebox/

The App Store version runs in Apple's App Sandbox, so it can only read and write inside its own container. It cannot see the rest of your disk.

What TimeBox never does

It does not transmit your tasks, timings, or usage anywhere. It does not read your files, contacts, calendar, location, camera, microphone, or clipboard. It does not show ads, and it has no in-app purchases. No data is sold or shared with anyone, because none is gathered in the first place.

System permissions

Notifications
Used only to tell you a time block has ended. Notifications are generated on your Mac and delivered by macOS; no notification content is sent to a server.
Launch at login
Optional, off by default. When enabled, TimeBox registers itself with macOS as a login item. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings → General → Login Items.
Outgoing network access
The App Store version declares the standard network.client entitlement. This is required by the system web view TimeBox uses to draw its own interface — without it, the window renders blank. TimeBox itself opens no connections. It is listed here because you would otherwise find it in the app's entitlements and reasonably wonder why.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the program. To remove your data as well, delete the folder listed above for your version. There is no server-side copy, so nothing else needs to be requested or erased, and there is no account to close.

Children

TimeBox is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version of TimeBox ever collects or transmits anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the change will be visible in the public commit history.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to github.com/gigiheristiawan/timebox/issues. Issues are public, so please don't include anything private in one.
